Fine-tuning did not make the model more accurate on unseen generators. It made it separate the two classes further apart, and it corrected genuine photographs without costing anything on the fake ones.

The weakness stays on the page. Fine-tuning did not improve fake recall at all: it is 70.0% before and 70.0% after, so the model still misses about three fakes in every ten. The whole gain came from correcting genuine photographs, where recall went from 70.0% to 100.0%. A fake verdict from the fine-tune is therefore stronger evidence than a real one.

On quoting these numbers. The accuracy point estimates come from small samples and should not be quoted without that caveat. At n=20 in-distribution, one image is five percentage points, and the whole +15-point gain is three additional correct predictions; each per-generator cell is four images, so one image is 25 points. The fine-tuned checkpoint comes from a single training run with no seed variance, and the in-distribution explanation-diversity diagnostics were not recomputed on this subset.
