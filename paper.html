A KAUST Academy report dated 17 August 2026. Every figure on this site is taken from it.

Deepfake & AI-Generated Image Detection with Visual Explanation using Vision–Language Models. Abdulqader Alfawzan, Ahmed Sayegh, Muhannad Alfwazan, Marwan Alayed, Ziyad Alghamdi and Salman Khan. KAUST Academy, August 2026.
Read the report (PDF, 2.9 MB)

What it reports

  • Class separation nearly doubled, +0.400 to +0.699 — the distance between the class-conditional means, which moves whether or not a prediction crosses a threshold.
  • Out-of-distribution accuracy across ten unseen generators held at 92.5%, unchanged by fine-tuning, and every one of the forty explanations was unique.
  • In-distribution accuracy rose 70.0% to 85.0%, entirely by fixing real photographs: real recall 70.0% to 100.0%, fake recall unchanged at 70.0%.
  • Reading the probability at the verdict token rather than the greedy word put the best operating point at t = 0.98, not the 0.50 a naive reading assumes.
  • Trainable parameters 62,586,880, 0.799% of the model, with both vision towers and the fusion stack frozen and verified bit-identical.
